## 11. Citation & Attribution

Research papers, books, documentation, datasets, and third-party resources should be appropriately acknowledged.

When using external code or research implementations:

- Cite the original authors.
- Provide links to original publications.
- Respect the original licenses.
- Clearly distinguish original implementations from adapted code.
